Key areas of focus include the arrival of new hardware for testing, ongoing issues with [Mesh Networking](./mesh-networking.md) reliability, concerns regarding product durability, and scheduled development Agile Sprints to address critical fixes.

*   **Firmware Development Sprint:** A dedicated Agile Sprint for Firmware Development is scheduled for the current week. This sprint is critical for addressing identified software issues, including the [Meshsync](./meshsync.md) pairing problems, and ensuring product readiness.

## Related Concepts
*   **Agile Sprint:** A time-boxed period, typically one to four weeks, during which a team works to complete a defined set of work, as exemplified by the scheduled firmware sprint.
*   **Beta Testing:** A phase of product development where a nearly complete version of a product is tested by a limited group of users or internally to identify bugs and gather feedback before general release.
*   **Firmware Development:** The process of designing, writing, and testing the embedded software that controls a hardware device.
*   **IP54 Rating:** An Ingress Protection standard indicating a device's resistance to dust ingress (level 5) and splashing water (level 4).
*   **Mesh Networking:** A network topology where each node relays data for the network, allowing for robust and self-healing connections, though specific pairing issues have been noted.
*   **Quality Assurance:** The systematic process of ensuring that products meet specified requirements and standards, highlighted by the need for fixes before a demo and concerns over IP54.
