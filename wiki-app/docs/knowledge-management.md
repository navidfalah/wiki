# Knowledge Management

Knowledge Management (KM) encompasses the processes and strategies used to identify, create, represent, distribute, and enable the adoption of insights and experiences. Effective KM aims to prevent Information Fragmentation, facilitate synthesis, and ensure that valuable knowledge is accessible and actionable. The process often involves organizing disparate research, capturing fleeting thoughts, and structuring information for future use.

## Overview

The provided context illustrates a common challenge in personal knowledge management: the fragmentation of research and ideas across various open tabs, mental dumps, and unfinished thoughts. This state highlights the need for robust KM practices to consolidate information, identify connections, and move from raw data to synthesized knowledge. The act of documenting these fragmented pieces is a preliminary step towards better organization.
