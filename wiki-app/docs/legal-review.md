# Legal Review

## Overview

This page summarizes the findings of a legal review, primarily focusing on trademark clearance for several product names and co-marketing guidelines. The review was conducted by outside counsel for Aurora Labs and TeaBuddy, with specific feedback directed to Mira Chen and Alex Kim.

## Key Details

### Trademark Clearance

*   **MeshSync**:
    *   Preliminary clearance is granted.
    *   A caveat exists regarding potential similarities with other "mesh IoT" marks.
*   **TeaBuddy**:
    *   Preliminary clearance is granted specifically for use in "Class 21 kitchen gadgets."
*   **"Puck"**:
    *   Using "Puck" alone is considered problematic due to it being a "crowded mark."
    *   Recommendation: Use "TeaBuddy Puck" to ensure distinctiveness.
*   **"SteepSync"**:
    *   This mark has been internally rejected previously and should be avoided.
*   **"Nova"**:
    *   Using "Nova" alone conflicts with an existing technology mark.
    *   Recommendation: Use "Nova Widget" to differentiate.

### Co-marketing Guidelines

*   Co-marketing efforts are permissible.
*   A strict requirement for logo separation exists: logos must be separated by a minimum of 24 pixels.
