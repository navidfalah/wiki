# Investor Relations

## Overview

Investor Relations at [Aurora Labs](./aurora-labs.md) involves communicating company progress, challenges, and funding needs to investors. A draft investor update from July 2026, authored by CEO [Mira Chen](./mira-chen.md), highlighted significant traction with beta units and software releases, alongside challenges related to product specifications and competitive positioning. The update also outlined a specific funding request to support critical development and manufacturing needs.

## Key Details

### Traction and Progress
*   **[Nova Widget](./nova-widget.md) Beta Units**: 47 Nova Widget beta units are currently deployed in the field.
*   **[MeshSync](./meshsync.md) Release**: MeshSync version 0.3.8 has been shipped, including a critical rejoin fix that unblocks 8-node deployments.
*   **Investor Impression**: A demonstration of the wiki compiler, which included a mention of the "[Karpathy pattern](./karpathy-pattern.md)," positively impressed a seed investor.

### Challenges
*   **IP Rating Tooling**: The $8,000 cost for IP65 tooling has been deferred. Beta units are currently shipping with an IP54 rating, accompanied by clear language regarding splash resistance.
*   **Competitive Landscape**: [SenseNode](./sensenode.md)'s [SN-400](./sn-400.md) product continues to hold the dominant narrative for outdoor waterproof solutions, boasting an IP67 rating.
*   **Documentation Discrepancy**: There is a contradiction in documentation regarding battery life claims, stating both 2 years and 18 months. A power budget spreadsheet is planned for publication in Q3 to clarify this.

### Strategic Discussions
*   **[TeaBuddy](./teabuddy.md) [Co-marketing](./co-marketing.md)**: [Alex Kim](./alex-kim.md) from TeaBuddy reached out to discuss potential co-marketing opportunities at [Maker Faire](./maker-faire.md). This initiative has been deferred until the Aurora beta product officially ships.
*   **Firmware Collaboration**: There has been no firmware merge with TeaBuddy. A joke about the [CR2032](./cr2032.md) supply chain was shared during discussions.

### Funding Ask
*   **[Bridge Funding](./bridge-funding.md)**: Aurora Labs is seeking $500,000 in bridge funding.
*   **Funding Allocation**: This funding is specifically requested for:
    *   Injection mold tooling.
    *   Hiring 2 Full-Time Equivalent (FTE) firmware engineers.
