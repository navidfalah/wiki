# Funding

## Overview

Funding refers to the financial resources sought or obtained by Aurora Labs to support its operations, product development, and growth. As of a July 2026 investor update draft, Aurora Labs was actively seeking bridge funding to address specific development needs and challenges.

## Key Details

*   **Bridge Funding Request:** Aurora Labs is seeking a $500,000 bridge funding round.
*   **Purpose of Funding:** The requested funds are earmarked for two primary objectives:
    *   Acquisition of an injection mold.
    *   Hiring of two Full-Time Equivalent (FTE) firmware engineers.
*   **Context of Request:** The funding request is outlined in an internal investor update draft, which also details recent traction and ongoing challenges faced by Aurora Labs.
*   **Challenges Impacting Funding Needs:**
    *   **Tooling Deferral:** An $8,000 cost for IP65 tooling was deferred, leading to the shipment of IP54 beta units with clear splash-resistance language. This indicates a need for capital to improve product ruggedness.
    *   **Competitive Landscape:** The competitor SenseNode SN-400 currently holds a stronger outdoor waterproof narrative with an IP67 rating, highlighting a gap Aurora Labs aims to close.
    *   **Internal Documentation:** An internal contradiction exists regarding battery life claims (2 years vs. 18 months), which Aurora Labs plans to resolve by publishing a power budget spreadsheet in Q3.

*   **Bridge Funding:** A short-term loan or investment that "bridges" a company to its next major financing round.
*   **Injection Molding:** A manufacturing process used to produce parts by injecting molten material into a mold, critical for product enclosures and durability.
*   **Firmware Development:** The process of creating software that controls specific hardware, with FTE firmware indicating a need for dedicated engineering resources.
*   **IP Ratings (Ingress Protection):** Standards (e.g., IP54, IP65, IP67) that classify the degree of protection provided against intrusion of solids and liquids.
*   **Co-marketing:** Collaborative marketing efforts between two or more companies.
*   **Investor Relations:** The strategic management of communication between a company and its investors.
*   **Product Development Challenges:** Obstacles encountered during the creation and refinement of a product, such as tooling costs or competitive features.
