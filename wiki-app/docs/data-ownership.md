# Data Ownership

## Overview
Data ownership is a foundational principle guiding the development of new sensor technologies, particularly for applications like gardening. The core idea is to empower users, such as gardeners, to have full control and ownership over the data collected by their devices. This approach emphasizes transparency and user autonomy regarding personal and environmental data.

## Key Details
*   **Core Principle:** The initiative aims to provide "Open Sensors for gardeners who own their data," ensuring users retain control over the information generated by their devices.
*   **Product Context:** This principle is central to the design and purpose of the Nova Widget, a soil moisture and temperature sensor.
*   **Technical Considerations:**
    *   The Nova Widget utilizes MeshSync technology to extend its range, which is relevant to how data is collected and potentially distributed, thus impacting ownership models.
    *   The nRF52840 microcontroller (MCU) has been selected for the Nova Widget.
    *   A key performance target for the device is an 18-month battery life.
*   **Project Origin:** The concept of data ownership for open sensors was a key discussion point during a kickoff meeting held on 2026-06-15.
