# LLM Applications

## Overview
LLM Applications leverage Large Language Models (LLMs) to automate and enhance various tasks, particularly in [Knowledge Management](./knowledge-management.md) and content generation. A prominent application involves transforming unstructured data, such as support tickets, into structured and accessible knowledge base content like FAQ pages.

## Key Details
*   **Support Ticket Summarization**: A primary application of LLMs is to summarize support tickets, converting the detailed interactions and resolutions into concise Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) pages. This process automates the creation and maintenance of self-service knowledge bases.
*   **Wiki Automation Patterns**: The "karpathy llm wiki pattern" is referenced as a model for integrating LLMs into wiki environments, suggesting broader applications for content generation, organization, and maintenance within knowledge bases.
