# Kickoff Meeting

## Overview
This kickoff meeting, held on 2026-06-15, established the foundational vision for a new product line focused on Open Sensors for gardeners, emphasizing user [Data Ownership](./data-ownership.md). Key outcomes included the introduction of the "[Nova Widget](./nova-widget.md)" product concept and initial technical decisions regarding hardware and battery life targets.

## Key Details
*   **Date:** 2026-06-15
*   **Attendees:**
    *   Mira Chen
    *   Jonah Park
*   **Core Vision:** To develop open sensors for gardeners, ensuring users retain full ownership of their data.
*   **Product Idea: Nova Widget**
    *   **Functionality:** A sensor designed to measure soil moisture and temperature.
    *   **Connectivity:** Planned to utilize [MeshSync](./meshsync.md) technology to achieve extended range and reliable communication through [Mesh Networking](./mesh-networking.md).
*   **Technical Decisions:**
    *   **Microcontroller (MCU):** The nRF52840 was selected as the primary microcontroller.
    *   **Battery Target:** A critical design goal is to achieve an 18-month battery life for the device.
