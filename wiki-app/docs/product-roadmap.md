# Product Roadmap

## Overview

A product roadmap outlines the strategic direction and planned evolution of a product over time, detailing features, improvements, and key initiatives. This page synthesizes information regarding the roadmap for the [Aurora Nova Widget](./aurora-nova-widget.md), particularly concerning its waterproofing capabilities and feature focus, as well as distinctions from other products.

## Key Details

### Aurora Nova Widget Roadmap

*   **Waterproofing**:
    *   The Aurora Nova Widget currently has an IP54 rating, meaning it is protected from limited dust ingress and water spray from any direction.
    *   There is a plan to upgrade the waterproofing to IP65 on the product roadmap.
    *   The current IP54 rating, rather than a higher IP67 rating like some competitors, is attributed to a Cost/Tooling Tradeoff.
    *   A customer experienced unit failure due to rain when installed in a raised bed, highlighting the need for improved waterproofing.
*   **Feature Focus**:
    *   The beta phase of the Aurora Nova Widget is primarily focused on developing Local Mesh capabilities and Open Export functionalities.
*   **Documentation**:
    *   An update to the product comparison page, likely to clarify waterproofing differences, is scheduled for **sprint 15**.

### Product Comparisons

*   **SenseNode**:
    *   The [SenseNode](./sensenode.md), a competitor product (SN-400), offers an IP67 rating, providing superior protection against dust and immersion in water.
*   **TeaBuddy Puck**:
    *   The TeaBuddy Puck is a different product designed for splash-resistant kitchen use and is not intended for outdoor or waterproof applications.

## Related Concepts

*   **IP Ratings (Ingress Protection)**: Standards defining the sealing effectiveness of electrical enclosures against intrusion from foreign bodies (dust, dirt) and moisture.
    *   **IP54**: Protected from limited dust ingress and water spray from any direction.
    *   **IP65**: Dust tight and protected against water jets from any direction.
    *   **IP67**: Dust tight and protected against immersion in water up to 1 meter for 30 minutes.
*   **Cost/Tooling Tradeoff**: A common consideration in product development where design choices are influenced by manufacturing costs and available tooling.
*   **Local Mesh**: A network topology where devices communicate directly with each other within a local area.
*   **Open Export**: The ability to easily extract data from a device or system in a standard, accessible format.
