## Related Entities

*   **nRF52840**: A low-power Bluetooth 5, Bluetooth Mesh, Thread, Zigbee, and 2.4 GHz proprietary multi-protocol System-on-Chip (SoC) often used in IoT devices.
*   **CR2032**: A common 3V lithium coin cell battery, widely used in small electronic devices.
*   **Capacitive Soil Probe**: A type of sensor used to measure soil moisture content by detecting changes in capacitance.
*   **Mesh Networks**: A network topology where each node relays data for the network, often used in IoT for extended range and reliability.
*   **LoRaWAN**: A Low Power Wide Area Network (LPWAN) specification for wireless battery-operated "things" in a regional, national, or global network.

## Related Concepts

*   **Sleep Modes**: Power-saving states in microcontrollers and other electronic devices designed to minimize energy consumption when idle.
*   **Battery Discharge Curve**: A graph illustrating the voltage of a battery over time as it discharges, providing insights into its capacity, internal resistance, and performance characteristics.
*   **Corrosion**: The gradual destruction of materials (typically metals) by chemical and/or electrochemical reaction with their environment, impacting device longevity and reliability.
*   **Power Efficiency**: The ratio of useful power output to total power input, a critical metric for battery-powered and energy-constrained electronic devices.
*   **Wireless Communication Protocols**: Standardized sets of rules for exchanging information over a wireless medium, such as Bluetooth, Zigbee, Thread, Mesh, and LoRaWAN.
