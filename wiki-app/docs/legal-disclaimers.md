# Legal Disclaimers

## Overview
Legal disclaimers outline important terms, conditions, and limitations related to product usage, [Beta testing](./beta-testing.md) agreements, and data handling. These disclaimers typically cover aspects such as confidentiality of proprietary information, safety guidelines for device operation, data privacy practices, and clarification regarding partnerships or endorsements.

## Key Details

### Confidentiality
*   Certain materials are considered confidential and must be protected. These include:
    *   [Beta firmware](./beta-firmware.md)
    *   [MeshSync source partial](./meshsync-source-partial.md)
    *   [Power budget spreadsheets](./power-budget-spreadsheets.md)

### Device Safety and Usage
*   The [Aurora Nova Widget](./aurora-nova-widget.md) is designed to be IP54 splash-resistant only.
*   Users are explicitly warned not to submerge the device in water.
*   Outdoor burial, similar to [SenseNode](./sensenode.md)-style installations, is not supported for the Aurora Nova Widget.

### Data Handling
*   Telemetry data generated by the device remains local by default.
*   Users have the option to configure an [MQTT export](./mqtt-export.md) for telemetry data, which is user-controlled.

### No Implied Endorsement or Partnership
*   A beta tester mentioning products like [TeaBuddy](./teabuddy.md) or other third-party items does not imply any partnership or endorsement by Aurora.
