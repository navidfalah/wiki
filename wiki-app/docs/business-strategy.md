# Business Strategy

## Overview

[Aurora Labs](./Aurora-Labs.md) employs a business strategy focused on differentiating its products through specific technical advantages, fostering community engagement, and strategic partnerships. Key elements include offering a subscription-free local mesh solution, leveraging [Open Firmware](./Open-Firmware.md), and carefully managing product development and marketing claims.

## Key Details

*   **[Competitive Advantage](./Competitive-Advantage.md) (Moat against [SenseNode](./SenseNode.md))**:
    *   Local mesh functionality without requiring a subscription.
    *   Partial open firmware.
    *   Strong community integrations.
*   **Product Development & Features**:
    *   **Battery Life**: Engineering estimates indicate 18 months of battery life for a system with ten nodes performing fifteen-minute reads. Marketing materials round this up to two years, with a detailed spreadsheet to be published for transparency.
    *   **Mesh Stability**: An issue with 8-node mesh configurations has been mitigated in version 0.3.8. For beta customers, a 6-node mesh is recommended.
    *   **[IP Rating](./IP-Rating.md)**: Products currently hold an IP54 rating for beta versions, with plans to achieve IP65 once tooling funding is secured.
*   **Partnerships**:
    *   A partnership with [TeaBuddy](./TeaBuddy.md) is strictly for [Co-marketing](./Co-marketing.md) purposes. There are no plans for a merger, and the collaboration was initiated through a personal connection ([Alex](./Alex.md)).
*   **Customer Recommendations**:
    *   Beta customers are advised to use a 6-node mesh configuration for optimal performance.

## Related Concepts

*   **Competitive Advantage**: Strategies employed to outperform competitors, such as subscription-free models and open firmware.
*   **Open Firmware**: A strategic choice to provide partial access to the device's firmware, potentially fostering community development and trust.
*   **[Community Integrations](./Community-Integrations.md)**: Leveraging community involvement to enhance product value and ecosystem.
*   **[Product Development Lifecycle](./Product-Development-Lifecycle.md)**: Stages from issue mitigation (8-node mesh) to feature enhancement (IP rating upgrades).
*   **[Beta Testing](./Beta-Testing.md)**: The phase of product development involving early customers and specific recommendations (e.g., 6-node mesh).
*   **Co-marketing**: A partnership strategy focused on joint promotional activities without deeper integration.
*   **IP Rating**: An international standard for ingress protection, indicating a device's resistance to dust and water.
