# Specifications

## Overview

Specifications define the precise requirements and characteristics for products, systems, or processes. They serve as a critical reference for engineering, development, and quality assurance, often contrasting with [Marketing](./marketing.md) claims or initial operational [Default Settings](./default-settings.md). Maintaining accurate and up-to-date specifications is crucial for avoiding discrepancies and ensuring correct implementation.

## Key Details

*   **[Default Timing Specification](./default-timing-specification.md)**: The official [specification](./default-timing-specification.md) for a [default setting](./default-settings.md) is fifteen minutes.
*   **[Engineering Requirements](./engineering-requirements.md) vs. [Marketing](./marketing.md) Claims**:
    *   Engineering requires a minimum of eighteen months of operation at ten nodes.
    *   [Marketing](./marketing.md) may communicate a different figure, such as two years, which can diverge from the strict engineering specification.
