# Soil Probes

## Overview
Soil probes are essential tools for monitoring soil conditions, particularly moisture levels. However, a significant challenge, especially for capacitive soil probes, is corrosion, which can lead to premature failure. This issue is exacerbated in certain environmental conditions, impacting the longevity and reliability of various probe designs.

## Key Details
*   **Corrosion Issue**: Cheap capacitive soil probes are prone to failure within 6 to 9 months when used in Acidic Soil conditions. This corrosion significantly reduces their operational lifespan.
*   SenseNode SN-400: This device utilizes a coated probe design to mitigate corrosion. Replacement probes for the SenseNode SN-400 are available for $12.
*   Aurora Beta Probe: An experimental Aurora Beta Probe features a gold-flashed PCB design, intended to improve durability. Its long-term performance regarding corrosion resistance is currently untested.
*   **Context**: The issue of soil probe corrosion is relevant to hardware development, particularly for devices like the Nova Widget, and is a consideration for overall product reliability and Total Cost of Ownership.
