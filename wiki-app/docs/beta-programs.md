# Beta Programs

## Overview
Beta Programs are initiatives, often run by [Aurora Labs](./Aurora%20Labs.md), to test pre-release versions of products or features with a select group of users. These programs are crucial for identifying bugs, gathering feedback, and refining products before a general release. A notable example is the beta program for the [Aurora Nova Widget](./Aurora%20Nova%20Widget.md).

## Key Details
*   **Product Focus:** The Aurora Nova Widget has been a subject of beta testing, with participants invited in batches (e.g., [Batch 3](./Batch%203.md)).
*   **Firmware Version:** Beta participants are typically provided with specific [firmware 0.3.8](./Firmware%200.3.8.md) versions for testing, such as firmware 0.3.8 for the Nova Widget.
*   **Installation Requirements:** Specific instructions are often provided, such as flashing firmware 0.3.8 before connecting more than six [nodes](./Nodes.md) to the Aurora Nova Widget.
*   **Default Settings:** Important operational parameters, like the default [read interval](./Read%20Interval.md), are communicated to testers. For the Nova Widget, this is 15 minutes.
*   **Documentation:** Comprehensive [Documentation](./Documentation.md) is made available to beta testers, often compiled from raw sources and hosted on platforms like `wiki.auroralabs.example`.
*   **Issue Reporting:** Testers are encouraged to report any issues encountered. Primary channels include:
    *   [GitHub](./GitHub.md), specifically the `aurora-labs/meshsync` repository, often with a dedicated issue number (e.g., #442).
    *   Direct email support (e.g., `support@auroralabs.example`).

## Related Entities
*   **Aurora Labs:** The organization that runs and manages these beta programs.
*   **Aurora Nova Widget:** A specific product that has undergone beta testing.
*   **GitHub:** A platform used for tracking and reporting issues during beta programs.

## Related Concepts
*   **Firmware Flashing:** The process of updating the software on a device, a common task for beta testers.
*   **Issue Reporting:** The structured process for beta testers to document and submit bugs or feedback.
*   **Documentation:** The instructional and reference materials provided to beta testers.
*   **Beta Testing:** The overall process of testing pre-release software or hardware.

## Contradictions
*   **Read Interval:** Older documentation (e.g., an old PDF) may state an [hourly read interval](./Hourly%20Read%20Interval.md), which has been superseded by a 15-minute default read interval in newer beta firmware versions.
