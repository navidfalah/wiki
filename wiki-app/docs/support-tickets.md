# Support Tickets

## Overview

Support tickets serve as a record of customer inquiries and agent responses, providing a structured way to address user questions, troubleshoot issues, and offer product information. They often highlight common customer concerns and product comparisons.

## Key Details

A representative support ticket (#2099, dummy) illustrates a common customer inquiry regarding product suitability for specific environments:

*   **Customer Inquiry**: A customer sought clarification on the suitability of the Nova Widget versus the SenseNode SN-400 for outdoor use.
*   **Agent Response**:
    *   The SenseNode SN-400 is rated IP67, indicating high protection against dust and water ingress, making it suitable for outdoor environments.
    *   The Nova Widget is currently in beta and has an IP54 rating, suggesting it has less robust protection compared to IP67 devices.
    *   MeshSync is noted for not incurring any Cloud Fees.

## Related Entities

*   **Nova Widget**: A product mentioned in comparison for outdoor use, currently in beta with an IP54 rating.
*   **SenseNode SN-400**: A product highlighted for its IP67 rating, making it suitable for outdoor use.
*   **MeshSync**: A system or service noted for its lack of cloud fees.

## Related Concepts

*   **[IP Rating (Ingress Protection)](./IP Rating (Ingress Protection).md)**: A standard indicating the degree of protection provided by electrical enclosures against intrusion from solid objects (like dust) and water.
    *   **IP67**: Indicates complete protection against dust ingress and protection against immersion in water up to 1 meter for 30 minutes.
    *   **IP54**: Indicates protection against dust ingress (limited ingress permitted) and protection against water splashes from any direction.
*   **Cloud Fee**: A recurring charge associated with using cloud-based services or platforms.
