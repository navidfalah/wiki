# Battery Technology

## Overview

Battery technology, particularly in the context of low-power devices, involves considerations such as battery type, [Duty Cycles](./duty-cycles.md), and the accuracy of advertised battery life. The [CR2032 battery](./cr2032-battery.md) is a common choice for compact gadgets, but its real-world performance can vary significantly based on device usage patterns.

## Key Details

*   **CR2032 Battery Usage**: The [CR2032 battery](./cr2032-battery.md) is utilized in multiple products, indicating its suitability for certain low-power applications.
*   **Impact of Duty Cycles**: The actual lifespan of a battery, even the same model like the [CR2032 battery](./cr2032-battery.md), is heavily influenced by the device's "[Duty Cycles](./duty-cycles.md)." Different products, despite using identical batteries, can exhibit vastly different battery longevity due to varying operational patterns and power consumption.
*   **Battery Life Marketing**: There can be discrepancies between advertised battery life and real-world performance or internal estimates.

## Related Concepts

*   **[Duty Cycles](./duty-cycles.md)**: Refers to the operational pattern of a device, including how frequently it performs tasks, transmits data, or remains active, directly impacting battery drain.
*   **[Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE)](./bluetooth-low-energy-ble.md)**: A wireless technology often used in low-power devices, which can have different power consumption profiles compared to [Mesh Networking](./mesh-networking.md).
*   **[Mesh Networking](./mesh-networking.md)**: A network topology where devices relay data for each other, potentially affecting power usage depending on the device's role in the mesh.
*   **[IP67](./ip67.md)**: An Ingress Protection rating indicating a device's resistance to dust and water, which can influence design choices and potentially battery compartment sealing.
