# Product Information

## Overview
This page compiles various pieces of product information, primarily focusing on specifications and common inquiries related to the Aurora Nova Widget and its ecosystem. It addresses discrepancies found in public documentation and provides clarifications on product features and compatibility.

## Key Details

### Aurora Nova Widget Battery Specifications
*   **Correct Battery Type**: The Aurora Nova Widget uses a **CR2032** battery.
*   **Historical Discrepancy**: An earlier teardown blog by Alex incorrectly listed the battery type as CR2450. This blog was corrected on 2026-06-20, and the official wiki page has been updated to reflect the correct specification.

### Aurora Nova Widget Battery Life
*   **Factors Influencing Battery Life**: The actual battery life of the Aurora Nova Widget is variable and depends on several factors, including:
    *   The number of connected nodes.
    *   The read interval (default is 15 minutes).
*   **Upcoming Documentation**: Detailed information regarding power consumption and battery life expectations will be available in a forthcoming "Power Budget" document.

### Product Compatibility
*   **TeaBuddy Application**: The TeaBuddy product does not share an application with the Aurora Nova Widget. TeaBuddy is a distinct product from a different company.
