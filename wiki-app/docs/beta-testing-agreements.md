# Beta Testing Agreements

## Overview
Beta Testing Agreements are legal documents that outline the terms and conditions for individuals participating in the beta testing phase of a product or service. These agreements typically cover aspects such as confidentiality, product usage guidelines, data handling, and disclaimers regarding endorsements or partnerships. They are crucial for protecting intellectual property, ensuring proper use of pre-release products, and managing expectations between the developer and the beta tester.

## Key Details
Key provisions commonly found in Beta Testing Agreements include:

*   **Confidentiality**:
    *   Specific items designated as confidential include beta firmware, partial MeshSync source code, and power budget spreadsheets.
    *   Beta testers are typically required to keep these details private.
*   **Safety and Usage Guidelines**:
    *   Devices, such as the Aurora Nova Widget, may have specific safety limitations. For example, the device is noted as IP54 splash-resistant only, meaning it should not be submerged.
    *   Certain usage scenarios, like SenseNode-style outdoor burial, are explicitly not supported.
*   **Data Handling and Telemetry**:
    *   Telemetry data collected from the device remains local by default.
    *   Users may have the option to configure an optional MQTT export for their data.
*   **No Endorsement or Partnership Implied**:
    *   Beta testers are generally permitted to mention other products, such as TeaBuddy, but such mentions do not imply any partnership or endorsement from the product developer (e.g., Aurora).
