# Business Operations

## Overview

Business operations encompass the day-to-day activities and strategic initiatives undertaken by organizations to achieve their objectives. This includes Product Development, market traction, financial management, Quality Assurance (QA), and external collaborations. Key operational aspects highlighted include managing product development challenges, securing funding, ensuring documentation accuracy, and navigating Co-marketing opportunities.

## Related Concepts

*   **Beta Testing**: The phase of Product Development where pre-release versions are tested by external users.
*   **Bridge Funding**: Short-term financing used to "bridge" a company until a larger funding round or liquidity event.
*   **Co-marketing**: A collaborative marketing effort between two or more businesses.
*   **Compile Run**: The process of compiling code, often followed by updates to documentation or test data.
*   **Firmware Development**: The process of creating software embedded in hardware devices.
*   **IP Ratings (Ingress Protection)**: Standards defining the sealing effectiveness of electrical enclosures against intrusion from foreign bodies and moisture.
*   **Net Promoter Score (NPS)**: A metric used to gauge customer loyalty and satisfaction.
*   **Product Development**: The entire process of bringing a new product to market.
*   **Quality Assurance (QA)**: The process of ensuring products meet specified standards.
*   **Supply Chain**: The network involved in producing and delivering a product or service.
*   **Technical Documentation**: Written materials that explain the functionality, use, and architecture of a product or service.
