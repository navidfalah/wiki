# Business Operations

## Overview

Business operations encompass the day-to-day activities and strategic initiatives undertaken by organizations to achieve their objectives. This includes [Product Development](./Product_Development.md), market traction, financial management, [Quality Assurance (QA)](./Quality_Assurance_QA.md), and external collaborations. Key operational aspects highlighted include managing product development challenges, securing funding, ensuring documentation accuracy, and navigating [Co-marketing](./Co-marketing.md) opportunities.

## Key Details

### [Aurora Labs](./Aurora_Labs.md) Operations

*   **Traction & Product Development**:
    *   47 beta [Nova Widget](./Nova_Widget.md) units are currently in the field.
    *   [MeshSync](./MeshSync.md) 0.3.8 has been shipped, including a rejoin fix that unblocks 8-node deployments.
    *   A wiki compiler demo successfully impressed a seed investor, with a mention of the "karpathy pattern."
*   **Challenges**:
    *   **Tooling**: The $8,000 cost for IP65 tooling has been deferred. Beta units are shipping with IP54 splash-resistance, accompanied by clear language regarding its limitations.
    *   **Competition**: The [SenseNode SN-400](./SenseNode_SN-400.md) product maintains a stronger market narrative for outdoor waterproofing (IP67 rating).
*   **Funding**:
    *   Aurora Labs is seeking $500,000 in [Bridge Funding](./Bridge_Funding.md). This capital is intended for an injection mold and to hire two additional full-time equivalent (FTE) firmware engineers.
*   **Collaborations**:
    *   [Alex Kim](./Alex_Kim.md) of [TeaBuddy](./TeaBuddy.md) reached out regarding a potential [Co-marketing](./Co-marketing.md) initiative at Maker Faire. This collaboration has been deferred until the Aurora Labs beta product officially ships.
    *   There has been no firmware merge with TeaBuddy, though a joke about the [Supply Chain](./Supply_Chain.md) was shared.
*   **Documentation**:
    *   A power budget spreadsheet is planned for publication in Q3 to address existing [Technical Documentation](./Technical_Documentation.md) discrepancies.
